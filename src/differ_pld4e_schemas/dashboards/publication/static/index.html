PLD4Energy / Export for publication

Export an upload

The export is one ZIP with every file of the upload, and a copy of every target, substrate type and other entry it refers to in other uploads, such as the target and substrate database. The references are rewritten to point into the ZIP, so they work wherever it is published.

Publish on the central NOMAD

  1. Download the ZIP of the export above (it is kept for one day).
  2. Log in at nomad-lab.eu with your own account and create a new upload.
  3. Drop the ZIP into it; NOMAD unpacks and processes it.
  4. Check the entries, add coauthors and a dataset, and publish.

Until the central NOMAD can read this plugin's schemas, the logbooks, samples and targets show there as files and entries without their PLD4Energy sections.
